What to Expect Your First Time at a Kava Bar

Never been to a kava bar before? Here's exactly how it works, so you can walk in confident on your first visit to Tribal Kava Lounge in West Palm Beach.

1. It's a lounge, not a liquor bar

Everything we serve is non-alcoholic. Come for creamy kava clouds, fruit-forward kratom refreshers, and agua frescas — plus late-night lounge energy, music, study sessions, and community events.

2. Order at the counter

Tell our team a flavor style you like and whether you want kava, kratom (21+), or a no-kava/no-kratom agua fresca. We'll match you to something beginner-friendly. Not sure? Ask the Kava Guide before you arrive.

3. Sip responsibly

Start simple, don't mix with alcohol or other substances, and bring questions — that's what we're here for.

Good to know: Kratom products are for adults 21+ only, valid ID required. Do not mix kava or kratom with alcohol or other substances. Products are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

4. Stay for the vibe

Wi-Fi, comfortable seating, and drinks built for long sessions. See what's on at our Events page, or check the full menu.

Frequently asked

What happens at a kava bar?

You order a kava or kratom drink at the counter, find a seat, and relax. It's a social, alcohol-free lounge — people study, hang with friends, go on low-key dates, or catch events like open mic and DJ nights.

What should I order on my first visit?

Pick a flavor style. Sweet and creamy: Banana Pudding Kava Cloud or La Nube Tres Leches. Fruity and refreshing: Passionfruit Mint Kratom Refresher (21+) or Guava Lime Agua Fresca. Photo-ready: Blue Razz Coconut Cloud.

Does kava get you drunk?

No. Kava is not alcohol, so it does not get you drunk like beer, wine, or liquor. Some people may feel different after drinking kava, so we treat it as a responsible-use beverage.

Do I need to be 21?

Kratom products are 21+ only with valid ID. Our agua fresca refreshers contain no kava or kratom, so there's something for everyone in the group.
