Kava vs. kratom — the quick version

They're two different plants. Kava is a traditional root beverage we serve as creamy, dessert-style “Kava Clouds.” Kratom is a botanical leaf tea we serve in bright, fruit-forward refreshers for adults 21+. Full breakdown on our Kava vs. Kratom page.

Frequently asked

Is there a kava bar in West Palm Beach?

Yes — Tribal Kava Lounge is a kava and kratom bar at 770 S Military Trail, West Palm Beach, FL 33415, open Sunday through Thursday 8 AM to 12 AM and Friday and Saturday 8 AM to 1 AM.

What is a kava bar?

A kava bar is a non-alcoholic lounge that serves kava — a traditional botanical beverage made from the root of the kava plant — usually alongside kratom teas and other botanical drinks. It is a social, alcohol-free alternative to a regular bar.

Where can I get kava near me in Palm Beach County?

Tribal Kava Lounge serves West Palm Beach and nearby Lake Worth, Greenacres, Palm Springs, Wellington, Royal Palm Beach, Loxahatchee and Haverhill from its location on South Military Trail.

Are kava bars alcohol-free?

Yes. Kava and kratom drinks contain no alcohol. They are still responsible-use botanical beverages, so we do not recommend mixing them with alcohol or other substances, and kratom is 21+ only with valid ID.
