Non-Alcoholic Night-Out Ideas in West Palm Beach

Sober-curious, cutting back, or just over the bar scene? West Palm Beach has more to offer at night than you'd think — here are some alcohol-free ways to have a great time.

Hang at a kava lounge

A kava bar is the easy answer for a night out that doesn't revolve around alcohol. At Tribal Kava Lounge you get creamy kava clouds, fruit-forward kratom refreshers (21+), and agua frescas in a real lounge setting — open until midnight most nights and 1 AM on weekends.

Great for…

  • Date night without the bar-scene pressure.
  • Study or work sessions with Wi-Fi and a drink in hand.
  • Friend hangs — bring the crew, including anyone who doesn't want kava or kratom (agua frescas have neither).
  • Late-night lounge energy, open mic, and DJ nights — see Events.
Good to know: Kratom products are for adults 21+ only, valid ID required. Do not mix kava or kratom with alcohol or other substances. Products are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

Serving the whole area

We're at 770 S Military Trail and welcome guests from West Palm Beach, Lake Worth, Greenacres, Palm Springs, Wellington, Royal Palm Beach, Loxahatchee and Haverhill. New to it all? Start with our First-Time Guide.

Frequently asked

What is there to do at night in West Palm Beach without drinking?

Plenty — live music, waterfront walks, art and food events, and non-alcoholic lounges like Tribal Kava Lounge, where you can hang late over kava clouds and kratom refreshers instead of alcohol.

Is there a sober-friendly bar in West Palm Beach?

Yes. Tribal Kava Lounge is a fully non-alcoholic botanical lounge on South Military Trail — a social, alcohol-free alternative to a regular bar.

What's a good non-alcoholic date-night spot?

A kava lounge makes an easy low-pressure date: low lighting, good music, and drinks worth talking about, with zero liquor-bar chaos.
