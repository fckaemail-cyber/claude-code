The First-Time Guide

Never had kava or kratom? Start here.

Most people walk in with questions. This guide explains what we serve, how to choose your first drink, and how to enjoy responsibly.

Ask the Kava Guide View Menu

[ Photo — beginner-friendly drink lineup on the bar ]

1

You are not behind.

Kava and kratom are new to a lot of people. We built the menu to make the experience easier with familiar flavors, clear labels, beginner-friendly options, and a team that is happy to help.

2

What is kava?

Kava is a traditional plant-based beverage made from the root of the kava plant. Traditional kava has an earthy taste. Our Kava Clouds are designed to make the experience smoother and more familiar with creamy dessert-style flavors.

Read the full “What is kava?” explainer →

Safety note: Kava is not for everyone. Do not mix kava with alcohol or other substances. Talk to a qualified professional before using kava if you are pregnant, nursing, taking medications, or have health concerns.
3

What is kratom?

Kratom is a botanical tea made from kratom leaves. We serve it in brewed refresher-style drinks with fruit-forward flavors.

Kratom products are 21+ only. Valid ID required.

Read the full “What is kratom?” explainer →

Safety note: Kratom is intended for responsible adult use only. Do not use if pregnant, nursing, taking medications, or if you have health concerns without speaking with a qualified professional. Do not mix with alcohol or other substances.
4

Is this alcohol?

No. We are not a liquor bar. Our drinks are non-alcoholic, but kava and kratom products should still be treated as responsible-use botanical beverages.

5

What should I order first?

Sweet and creamy

Banana Pudding Kava Cloud or La Nube Tres Leches.

Fruity and refreshing

Passionfruit Mint Kratom Refresher (21+) or Guava Lime Agua Fresca.

6

First-time tips

  • Choose your flavor style: creamy, fruity, tropical, or bold.
  • Ask our team what is beginner-friendly.
  • Start simple.
  • Sip responsibly.
  • Do not mix with alcohol or other substances.
  • Bring questions. That is what we are here for.
