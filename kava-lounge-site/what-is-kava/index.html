Botanical Basics

What Is Kava?

The plain-English guide to the Pacific Islands’ most famous root beverage — and how we turned it into a dessert-style cloud.

[ Photo — kava root & traditional bowl beside a modern kava cloud ]

The short answer

Kava is a traditional plant-based beverage made from the root of the kava plant (Piper methysticum), a member of the pepper family grown across the Pacific Islands — Fiji, Vanuatu, Tonga, Samoa, and beyond.

A drink with centuries of tradition

In Pacific Island cultures, kava has long been prepared by grinding the root and mixing it with water, then sharing it from a communal bowl. It has played a role in ceremonies, gatherings, and everyday social life for generations. When you order kava with us, you’re tasting a modern spin on a very old social ritual.

What does kava taste like?

Honestly? Traditional kava is earthy. Some people love it; most first-timers need a bridge. That’s why we built Kava Clouds — creamy, dessert-style drinks like the Banana Pudding Kava Cloud and La Nube Tres Leches that layer familiar flavors over a smooth kava base.

Is kava alcohol?

No. Kava is not alcohol and does not get you drunk like beer, wine, or liquor. Some people may feel different after drinking kava, so we treat it as a responsible-use beverage. Do not mix kava with alcohol or other substances.

Safety note: Kava is not for everyone. Do not mix kava with alcohol or other substances. If you are pregnant, nursing, taking medications, or have health concerns, speak with a qualified professional before using kava. Products are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
