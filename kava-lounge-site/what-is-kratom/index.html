What Is Kratom?

A plain-English guide to the Southeast Asian botanical tea — and how we serve it in bright, fruit-forward refreshers.

Kratom products are for adults 21+ only. Valid ID required.

The short answer

Kratom is a botanical tea made from the leaves of the kratom tree (Mitragyna speciosa), a member of the coffee family that grows in Southeast Asia — including Thailand, Indonesia, and Malaysia — where the leaves have traditionally been brewed as tea.

How we serve it

We serve kratom as a brewed botanical tea in fruit-forward, refresher-style drinks — think Mango Chili Lime Kratomade, Passionfruit Mint, and Blackberry Dragonfruit Fizz. At Tribal Kava Lounge we also carry kratom tea, kratom extracts, and kratom shots. Flavor first, clearly labeled, and always 21+ with valid ID.

Is kratom alcohol?

No. Kratom is not alcohol and our lounge is fully non-alcoholic. It is still a responsible-use botanical beverage: do not mix kratom with alcohol or other substances, and do not treat it as a daily habit.

The house rules

  • Kratom products are for adults 21+ only. Valid ID required.
  • Do not mix kava or kratom with alcohol or other substances.
  • We do not recommend using kratom as a daily habit or to self-treat any health condition.
  • Products are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
Safety note: Kratom is intended for responsible adult use only. If you are pregnant, nursing, taking medications, or have health concerns, speak with a qualified professional before using kratom. Do not mix with alcohol or other substances.

No problem — our Agua Fresca Refreshers have no kava and no kratom, and the Kava Clouds offer a totally different botanical experience. Curious how the two compare? Read Kava vs. Kratom.
