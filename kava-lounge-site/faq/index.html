Frequently Asked Questions

Everything first-timers actually ask — answered simply. Can’t find yours? Ask the Kava Guide in the corner of your screen.

What is kava?

Kava is a traditional plant-based beverage made from the root of the kava plant, with a long history in Pacific Island culture. Traditional kava has an earthy taste — our Kava Clouds are creamy, dessert-style drinks designed to make the first sip smoother and more familiar.

Read the full kava explainer →

What is kratom?

Kratom is a botanical tea made from kratom leaves, traditionally brewed in Southeast Asia. We serve it in brewed, fruit-forward refresher-style drinks for adults 21+ with valid ID.

Read the full kratom explainer →

Is kratom 21+?

Yes. Kratom products are for adults 21+ only. Valid ID required — no exceptions.

Are the drinks alcoholic?

No. We are a non-alcoholic lounge, not a liquor bar. Kava and kratom drinks are alcohol-free but should still be treated as responsible-use botanical beverages. Do not mix kava or kratom with alcohol or other substances.

Does kava taste earthy?

Traditional kava does have an earthy taste on its own. That’s exactly why we created Kava Clouds — creamy, dessert-style drinks like Banana Pudding and La Nube Tres Leches that make the flavor smooth and familiar.

What should I order first?

Pick a flavor style and go from there:

Sweet and creamy: Banana Pudding Kava Cloud or La Nube Tres Leches.
Fruity and refreshing: Passionfruit Mint Kratom Refresher (21+) or Guava Lime Agua Fresca.
Photo-ready: Blue Razz Coconut Cloud.
Bold and unique: Dubai Chocolate Pistachio Kava.

See the full first-time guide →

Do you have drinks without kava or kratom?

Yes! Our Agua Fresca Refreshers — Guava Lime, Watermelon Cucumber Lime, and Jamaica Passionfruit — contain no kava and no kratom. Perfect for anyone in the group.

Can I bring friends who have never tried it?

Absolutely — first-timers are our favorite guests. Friends who want to skip kava and kratom can order an agua fresca, and our team will happily walk everyone through the menu.

Should I mix these drinks with alcohol?

No. Do not mix kava or kratom with alcohol or other substances.

Can pregnant or nursing customers use kava or kratom?

If you are pregnant, nursing, taking medications, or have health concerns, speak with a qualified professional before using kava or kratom. In the meantime, our agua frescas contain no kava or kratom.
