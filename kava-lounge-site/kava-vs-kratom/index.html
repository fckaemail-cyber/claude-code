Kava vs. Kratom: What’s the Difference?

Both are botanical beverages, but they come from different plants, traditions, and serving styles. Here is a simple first-timer breakdown.

🌊 Kava 🍃 Kratom
Plant source The root of the kava plant (Piper methysticum), a member of the pepper family. The leaves of the kratom tree (Mitragyna speciosa), a member of the coffee family.
Traditional origin Pacific Island cultures — Fiji, Vanuatu, Tonga, and beyond — where kava has centuries of ceremonial and social history. Southeast Asia — including Thailand, Indonesia, and Malaysia — where the leaves have traditionally been brewed as tea.
How we serve it Creamy, dessert-style “Kava Clouds” like Banana Pudding and La Nube Tres Leches. Brewed botanical tea in bright, fruit-forward refreshers like the Mango Chili Lime Kratomade.
Flavor profile Traditional kava is earthy on its own; our clouds layer in creamy, vanilla, fruit, and dessert notes. Brewed kratom tea has a bold, tea-like taste; our refreshers pair it with mango, passionfruit, mint, and citrus.
Age note Served to adult guests. Ask our team if you have questions. Kratom products are 21+ only. Valid ID required.
First-time recommendation Banana Pudding Kava Cloud or La Nube Tres Leches — creamy and familiar. Passionfruit Mint Kratom Refresher — bright, fruit-forward, and beginner-friendly (21+).
Responsible-use note Do not mix kava with alcohol or other substances. Not for everyone — if pregnant, nursing, taking medications, or if you have health concerns, speak with a qualified professional first. Responsible adult use only. Do not mix kratom with alcohol or other substances. If pregnant, nursing, taking medications, or if you have health concerns, speak with a qualified professional first.
Bottom line: different plants, different traditions, different drinks — and both live on our menu as flavor-first, non-alcoholic botanical beverages. Products are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
